Work & Study

Urban Parks and Public Open Spaces

2024–present · University of Glasgow

Using mobile phone app data to understand how residents and visitors use urban parks and public open spaces across the UK. The project develops scalable indicators of park usage and demand to support evidence-based greenspace management and planning policy.

Datasets

  • Li, L., & Sinclair, M. (2026). H3 Mobile Mobility Dataset for 55 major UK cities (2017–2024). University of Glasgow. Restricted access.
  • Li, L., & Sinclair, M. (2026). Home locations of mobile users for 55 major UK cities (2017–2024). University of Glasgow. Restricted access.

Geographies of Gambling

2023–2024 · University of Bristol

Examining the spatial distribution and temporal stability of gambling venues on British high streets, and the relationship between physical gambling environments and online gambling participation. Built a longitudinal dataset of gambling shops in Great Britain (2014–2023).

Dataset

  • Li, L., & Tranos, E. (2026). Gambling Shop POI Dataset for Great Britain, 2014–2023. UK Data Service.

Urban Mobility and Wi-Fi Data

2016–2023 · Beijing Normal University (PhD)

Developed methods for analysing crowd behaviour and tourist movement in urban open spaces using Wi-Fi probe data. Work includes trajectory semantics, population segmentation, and tourism attractiveness modelling, applied to Beijing’s Shichahai scenic area.

GALLANT: Urban Sustainability Indicators

2024–present · University of Glasgow

Part of Work Stream 3 of the GALLANT (Glasgow As a Living Lab Accelerating Novel Transformation) project. Using multi-source data to develop local indicators that assess Glasgow’s current sustainability across environmental, social, and urban dimensions.
